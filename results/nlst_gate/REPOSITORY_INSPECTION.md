# Repository inspection before implementing the gate

The current active cohort is `data/nlst/manifests/nlst_pilot_1000_balanced_{subjects,series,pairs}.csv`. `data/nlst` points to `/ssd2/domenico/datasets/nlst/`. The user explicitly approved using the verified cached cohort rather than the original approximately 2,000-patient request. Historical 1,460/2,000-patient predictor artifacts refer to different data snapshots and cannot be substituted for a matched COLIPRI cohort.

Patients use the manifest `patient_id` (clinical linkage uses `dicom_patient_id`). Scans use `series_uid` and `screening_round`/`timepoint` T0/T1/T2, with `embedding_index` linking cache rows. Clinical time uses `scr_days0/1/2`, relative to randomization; deidentified calendar dates are not used to estimate follow-up intervals.

Existing label builders are `scripts/build_nlst_prognostic_labels.py` and `scripts/build_nlst_diagnostic_labels.py`. Prognostic targets are scan-landmarked `future_cancer_1y/2y/3y`; cancer event, event/censor duration, first diagnosis day, and quality flags are available. Diagnoses on/before a landmark and inadequate follow-up are excluded/masked. Mortality endpoints are unavailable. Existing diagnostic targets are isolation suspicion, nodule ≥4mm, emphysema, and fibrosis/scarring. Raw lesion growth/involution/preexistence codes exist, but no validated local scan-level significant-change definition was found. Current significant abnormalities and comparison suspicion must not be called longitudinal change.

`scripts/run_colipri_decodability.py` performs official frozen COLIPRI extraction from QC-selected native DICOM HU with RAS orientation, 2mm resampling, HU clamping, and 192³ crop/pad. It exposes `encode_image(pool=False, project=False, normalize=False)` as `[864,24,24,24]` dense features. These are cached, as are 864-dimensional mean/max embeddings and official projected 768-dimensional attention embeddings. The gate preserves the raw mean representation used by previous prognostic runs.

`scripts/run_colipri_native_spatial_token_world_model.py` already caches native `[2984,64,864]` spatial tokens. These are 4×4×4 block means of the pre-pooling dense grid, without channel PCA; averaging them reproduces the mean global embeddings. The gate reuses this cache rather than rerunning COLIPRI, and verifies equivalence and row alignment across all scans. Spatial coarsening is explicitly documented.

Existing prognostic heads include linear probes, small MLPs, `PrognosticAdapter`, and spatial attention/gated heads. Existing `build_pairs` and masked multitask BCE are reused. Existing global forecasting objective A was selected by validation MSE in `outputs/colipri_latent_world_model_ablation/selection.json`; cached predictions and matching pair manifests exist for five seeds. This predictor is reused for G3 without retraining a world model.

Lightning, CSV logging, and W&B are already configured. The new gate uses Lightning with restored validation-best checkpoints and offline W&B logs. Existing patient assignments in `outputs/colipri_decodability/patient_splits.csv` have 599/200/200 patients; unique patient assignments and scan-level consistency are verified before training. They are preserved, rather than replaced with a new 70/15/15 split. New neural training seeds are 42, 123, 2026.

The subset is cancer-enriched (500/1,000 selected; 499/999 cached), and its test patients have already appeared in earlier research experiments. COLIPRI pretraining also included NLST images. This gate assesses representation decodability on a development cohort; independent confirmation and population calibration are separate requirements.
